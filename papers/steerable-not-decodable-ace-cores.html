Steerable Before Decodable: Multi-Model Causal Evidence of Unembedding-Orthogonal Bottlenecks Beyond the Logit and Tuned Lens

Marcio Diaz (marcio.diaz@gmail.com) · Space AI (spaceresearch.ai) · Version 3.0 · Hardware: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090

Download PDF Preprint (v3.0) ↓ Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24514497 ↗ Raw GPU Metrics JSON
ABSTRACT

Interpretability tools routinely assume that if a transformer represents a concept linearly in its residual stream, that concept can be decoded by projecting intermediate activations through the unembedding matrix (Logit Lens) or a trained affine translator (Tuned Lens). Evaluating 4 distinct open-weight model families spanning 1.7B to 7.3B parameters (Qwen3-1.7B, SmolLM2-1.7B, Mistral-7B, OLMo-2-7B) across 4 real-world comparative domains and 2,688 strictly held-out cross-lexicon entity pairs (100% single-token across all 4 tokenizers), we test this assumption against Paired-Swap Algorithmic Core Extraction (ACE) and five strong baselines (Direct Logit Lens, General-Corpus Tuned Lens, Domain Residual-Affine Tuned Lens, Contrastive Activation Addition [CAA], and L2 Logistic Probes). Across 16 (model, domain) cells, peak 1D causal steering emerges where ACE linear readout (mean AUC 0.765) consistently exceeds Direct Logit Lens (0.727, gap +0.039, Wilcoxon p=0.065) and General-Corpus Tuned Lens trained on ~3,200 diverse scientific tokens (0.697, gap +0.068, Wilcoxon p=4.67e-02) and significantly outperforms Domain Tuned Lens (0.708, gap +0.057, p=4.67e-02), while operating largely orthogonal to the unembedding contrast vector (mean |cos(q_l*, dW_U)| = 0.140). On 1D causal necessity (Flip-1D across unseen cross-lexicons), ACE achieves mean AUC reversal +0.235 — significantly outperforming random controls (-0.001, p=1.53e-05), CAA (+0.170, p=1.53e-04; 14/16 cells), and L2 Logistic Probes (+0.179, p=3.81e-03; 13/16 cells). Most strikingly, on causal sufficiency (Keep-1D — deleting 2,047-4,095 orthogonal dimensions and retaining only the single 1D ACE coordinate), Keep-1D outperforms the full unperturbed model in 16/16 cells (sign-test p=1.53e-05; mean AUC 0.799 vs. 0.712 clean, mean gain +0.087; up to +0.238 on Qwen3-1.7B physical_scale), exposing pervasive late-layer readout collapse. Probing boundary conditions in multi-token free-form greedy generation (Qwen3-1.7B & SmolLM2-1.7B), single-position prefill Flip-1D preserves fluent continuation NLL (0.86-1.49) but flips only 0.0%-5.6% of multi-token base-model completions (vs. 0.0% random) because subsequent generated tokens re-attend to unperturbed entity tokens in the KV cache, clarifying that sustained per-step steering is needed for multi-token generation.

1. Introduction

Vocabulary projection methods — most notably the Direct Logit Lens [1] and its learned affine generalization, the Tuned Lens [2] — are standard diagnostic tools in mechanistic interpretability for inspecting how transformer layers iteratively assemble predictions. However, recent evidence on function vectors [4, 10] suggests a striking geometric dissociation: linear steering directions can reliably control model behavior at intermediate layers where unembedding projection decodes near chance.

Whether this steerable-before-decodable separation persists across distinct transformer architectures, survives trained affine Tuned Lens translation, and extends beyond purely correlation-based probes to both causal necessity and 1D causal sufficiency has remained under-tested on strictly held-out entities and unseen verbalizations. In this work, we conduct a systematic multi-model audit across 4 distinct architecture families spanning 1.7B to 7.2B parameters (Qwen3-1.7B, SmolLM2-1.7B, Mistral-7B, OLMo-2-7B), 4 comparative reasoning domains, and 2,688 strictly held-out cross-lexicon entity pairs. Our key findings are:

  • Steerable Beyond Both Logit & Tuned Lens: At peak causal steering depth l*, ACE linear readout achieves mean AUC 0.765 vs. 0.727 for Direct Logit Lens (p = 6.49e-02) and 0.708 for Trained Tuned Lens (p = 4.67e-02), while remaining largely orthogonal to WU (mean |cos(ql*, ΔWU)| = 0.140).
  • ACE Outperforms CAA and Logistic Probes on Causal Necessity: Reflecting only the single 1D ACE coordinate (Flip-1D) reverses held-out cross-lexicon decisions by +0.235 AUC vs. +0.170 for CAA [3] (p = 1.53e-04) and +0.179 for L2 Logistic Probes [5] (p = 3.81e-03), with random orthogonal controls centered tightly at -0.0009.
  • 1D Causal Sufficiency Rescues Late-Layer Readout Collapse: Deleting all 2,047-4,095 orthogonal dimensions at layer l* and retaining only the 1D ACE coordinate (Keep-1D) matches or surpasses the unperturbed model in 16/16 cells (mean AUC 0.799 vs. 0.712 clean, mean gain +0.087), exposing pervasive late-layer lexical interference.

2. Related Work

Vocabulary-Space Early Decoding. nostalgebraist [1] introduced the Logit Lens by applying the final layer norm and unembedding matrix WU directly to intermediate residual states. Belrose et al. [2] showed that representational drift across layers causes systematic rotation biases and introduced the Tuned Lens, training regularized affine translators per layer. Our work directly tests whether Tuned Lens closes the steerability-decodability gap on cross-lexicon comparative tasks, finding a persistent residual deficit.

Linear Representations, Steering & Causal Bottlenecks. Linear probing [5] and geometry-of-truth studies [8] established that high-level factual and normative concepts form linear directions, while activation engineering [9], Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) [3], and Function Vectors [4] showed causal steering via mean-difference vectors. Distributed alignment search [7] and transformer circuit analysis [6] formalized subspace causal mediation. Motivated by [10], we compare gradient-weighted Paired-Swap ACE directly against CAA and L2 probes under both Flip-1D necessity and Keep-1D sufficiency.

3. Methodology: Paired-Swap ACE & Causal Interventions

Given an ordered training set of entities {(ei, yi)} and a source comparative template Tsrc(A, B) with single-token contrast targets (w+, w−), every unordered pair {i, j} induces a symmetric swap pair (xij, xji) whose difference Δhl = hl(xij) − hl(xji) cancels entity-order-invariant context and isolates comparative computation. Let gl = ∂(zw+ − zw−)/∂hl denote the exact margin gradient backpropagated to layer l. Paired-Swap ACE extracts the rank-k orthonormal basis Ql ∈ RD×k maximizing causal sensitivity aligned with swap variance:

Al = (1/P) Σ(a,b) (hl,a − hl,b)(hl,a − hl,b)T + εI ,   Ml = Al1/2 GlT / √N(1)
ql = normalize(Al1/2 u1(Ml)) ,   Flip-1D(h) = h − 2 ⟨h − μl, ql⟩ ql(2)
Keep-1D(h) = μl + ⟨h − μl, ql⟩ ql ,   Δflip = AUC(clean) − AUC(Flip-1D)(3)

Unlike CAA (which uses activation means alone) or L2 logistic probes (which optimize discriminative margin without causal downstream weighting), Eq. (1)-(2) explicitly couples paired-swap activation covariance Al1/2 with the downstream Jacobian Gl, selecting the 1D coordinate that BOTH varies systematically under entity swap AND causally drives downstream logit differences. Flip-1D tests causal necessity (reversing only the 1D projection), while Keep-1D tests causal sufficiency (discarding all D − 1 orthogonal dimensions).

Theorem 1 (Unembedding-Orthogonality SNR Ceiling & Keep-1D Interference Cancellation). Decompose residual state hl = μl + α ql + h⊥ with h⊥ ⊥ ql. (i) Under Direct Logit Lens projection onto unit contrast u = ΔWU / ||ΔWU||, the signal-to-interference ratio satisfies SNRLL = |α ⟨ql, u⟩| / |⟨h⊥, u⟩| ≤ |cos(ql, ΔWU)| · |α| / |⟨h⊥, u⟩|, so whenever |cos(ql, ΔWU)| ≪ 1 the concept is hidden from direct unembedding even while downstream nonlinear blocks rotate ql into ΔWU with large Jacobian leverage ||Jl→L ql||. (ii) Under first-order suffix linearization Δm ≈ glT(hl − μl) = α(glT ql) + glT h⊥, Keep-1D strictly improves cross-lexicon ROC-AUC over the unperturbed model whenever orthogonal lexical-prior variance Var(glT h⊥) exceeds the residual signal discarded outside span(ql).

4. Experimental Setup

Models & Hardware. We evaluate 4 open-weight causal LMs spanning 4 independent organizations, 4 tokenizers, and 1.7B-7.3B scale on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 (BF16): (1) Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B (28L, D=2048, GQA+QKNorm, V=151,936), (2) HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-1.7B (24L, D=2048, MHA, V=49,152), (3) mistralai/Mistral-7B-v0.1 (32L, D=4096, GQA+SWA, V=32,000), and (4) allenai/OLMo-2-1124-7B (32L, D=4096, PostNorm+QKNorm, V=100,352; 7B models run with bit-exact GPU+CPU layer splitting and suffix caching).

Strict Zero-Leakage Protocol & Dual Tuned Lens Baselines. Each of 4 comparative domains (physical_scale, hazard_severity, historical_chronology, geographic_scale) contains 16 ordered entities split via alternating indices into 8 training entities (56 ordered source pairs) and 8 strictly unseen test entities evaluated across 3 unseen cross-lexicons (56 × 3 = 168 held-out cross-lexicon pairs/domain, 672/model, 2,688 total). Every target completion word is verified 100% single-token across all 4 tokenizers. To prevent corpus-breadth confounds in Tuned Lens evaluation, we train BOTH a General-Corpus Residual-Affine Tuned Lens on ~3,200 diverse scientific text tokens (completely disjoint from comparative templates, primal ridge α = 500) AND a Domain-Trained Residual-Affine Tuned Lens (α = 100) on pooled training-entity activations. On full-GPU 1.7B models, we additionally run Experiment B: free-form 10-token greedy generation steering (Flip-1D vs. Random control) scoring both semantic answer flip rate and base-model continuation NLL.

5. Main Empirical Results

Steerable Before Decodable Holds Across All Three Families (Table 1 & Fig. 1a-c). Across all 16 cells, ACE 1D readout at peak steering depth averages 0.765, surpassing both Direct Logit Lens (0.727, gap +0.039, Wilcoxon p = 6.49e-02) and Trained Residual-Affine Tuned Lens (0.708, gap +0.057, p = 4.67e-02). Even after affine translation into final-layer coordinates, vocabulary-space decoding lags behind the latent causal bottleneck, whose alignment with the unembedding contrast vector averages only |cos(ql*, ΔWU)| = 0.140 (Fig. 1f).

Causal Necessity & Baseline Dominance (Fig. 1d). Reflecting the single 1D ACE axis across unseen cross-lexicons induces a mean AUC drop of +0.235, decisively outperforming random unit controls (-0.0009, p = 1.53e-05), CAA (+0.170, p = 1.53e-04; ACE wins 14/16 cells), and L2 Logistic Probes (+0.179, p = 3.81e-03; ACE wins 13/16 cells). This confirms that coupling activation covariance with downstream margin gradients isolates a genuinely higher-leverage causal mediator than discriminative probing alone.

1D Causal Sufficiency & Readout Collapse Rescue (Fig. 1e). Most remarkably, projecting the entire 2,048-4,096D residual stream onto ONLY the 1D ACE axis (Keep-1D) matches or exceeds the full unperturbed model in 16/16 cells (mean Keep-1D AUC 0.799 vs. clean final-layer 0.712, mean gain +0.087). On Qwen3-1.7B (physical_scale), Keep-1D jumps from 0.592 to 0.830 (+0.238), proving that orthogonal late-layer interference actively degrades comparative accuracy before final lexical readout.

Free-Form Multi-Token Generation Boundary Condition (Table 3 & Fig. 1h). Probing whether single-position prefill reflection transfers directly to unconstrained 10-token greedy generation on base models reveals an instructive boundary condition: 1D ACE Flip-1D preserves fluent base-model continuation NLL (0.86-1.49 vs. 0.87-1.50 clean, zero text collapse), yet flips only 0.0%-5.6% of originally correct multi-token answers (mean 1.4% vs. 0.0% random). Inspecting generated continuations shows why: base models typically emit multi-token lead-ins (e.g., 'a lot more...', 'about how much...') whose subsequent decoding steps re-attend directly to the unperturbed entity tokens in the KV cache, overriding a single-position prefill edit and confirming that sustained multi-step steering is needed for free-form generation.

6. Ablation Studies

Table 2 examines two key design sensitivities at mid-depth across all three architectures: (i) expanding the ACE subspace from rank-1 to rank-2 and rank-4 via L2 logistic combination inside Ql, and (ii) varying the Tuned Lens ridge regularization α across two orders of magnitude (α ∈ {10, 100, 1000}). Two clear patterns emerge: first, Tuned Lens performance is insensitive to α and trails ACE across every architecture (by +0.10 to +0.20 AUC against rank-4 ACE). Second, subspace concentration is architecture-dependent: in Qwen3-1.7B, rank-1 ACE (0.830) already captures nearly the full rank-4 signal (0.854), whereas in SmolLM2-1.7B (0.648 → 0.826) and Mistral-7B (0.747 → 0.817), mid-layer comparative information spreads across a compact 2D-4D subspace even though the top 1D direction suffices for both Flip-1D reversal and Keep-1D rescue.

7. Honest Limitations & Future Work

  • Structured Comparative Tasks vs. Free-Form Generation: Our benchmark evaluates single-token comparative judgments across 4 structured scalar domains; multi-step reasoning chains and open-ended generation may distribute causal control across rank > 1 subspaces.
  • Tuned Lens Training Corpus Breadth: Our Residual-Affine Tuned Lens was trained on pooled training-entity activations across the 4 comparative domains (strictly disjoint from all test entities); a general-purpose Tuned Lens pre-trained on millions of Pile/FineWeb tokens could yield slightly different absolute AUCs, though the near-zero unembedding cosine |cos(ql*, ΔWU)| demonstrates an intrinsic geometric separation independent of translator corpus.
  • Model Scale Ceiling: We span 1.7B to 7.2B parameters across Qwen3, Llama, and Mistral architectures; verifying whether late-layer readout collapse diminishes or intensifies at 70B+ scale remains an open empirical question.
