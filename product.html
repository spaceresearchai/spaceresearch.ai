07 · Field Notes

Questions &plain answers.

Do I need to install anything to start using Space AI?
No. Open Space Chat in your browser or point any OpenAI-compatible app directly at https://api.spaceresearch.ai/v1 right away. Space Desktop and the 1-line CLI are optional tools for local spillover, coding-tool fallback, and Auto-Paused hardware sharing.
How do I know I am getting the genuine model?
Every active computer is continuously checked in the background to confirm it is running the authentic open model it advertises. Any station that fails a quality check is automatically dropped from routing.
Do I need crypto or an expensive GPU to start?
No. Try Space Chat free in your browser right now with no account, use free welcome credits via the CLI, or set a simple pay-as-you-go cap ($1–$50) with no monthly subscription.
Will sharing idle time interfere with gaming or work?
No. Auto-Pause monitors GPU and system activity and pauses background work the moment you start gaming or running demanding apps. Press Ctrl+C anytime to stop.
