07 · Technical Notes

Questions &direct answers.

Do I need to install anything to use Space AI tools?
No. Open Space Chat in your browser or point any OpenAI-compatible client directly at https://api.spaceresearch.ai/v1 right away. The 1-line CLI is optional for local coding tools and Auto-Paused hardware sharing.
How do you verify that nodes serve genuine open models?
Every active computer is continuously checked in the background to confirm it is running the authentic open weights it advertises. Any node that fails an integrity check is automatically removed from routing.
How does your Quoridor research connect to the network?
Our exact Quoridor theorem provers and single-workstation 2,600+ Elo self-play engines test the same core principles powering Space AI: verifiable reasoning without bluffs, extreme efficiency per watt, and open reproducibility. Read the full note on our Research page.
Will contributing idle hardware interfere with my own work?
No. Auto-Pause monitors GPU and system activity and pauses background serving the moment you start working or gaming. Press Ctrl+C anytime to stop.
