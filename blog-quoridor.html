NOTE 02 · FORMAL SEARCH & GAME THEORY 6 min read

Solving the Maze: Exact Proofs and Neural Grandmasters in Quoridor

Using Quoridor as a clean scientific laboratory for exact theorem discovery, adversarial graph topology, and single-workstation neural self-play optimization.

ABSTRACT

We study 5×5 and 7×7 Quoridor across two coupled fronts: exact computer-verified minimax proof search and high-throughput neural self-play. On the formal side, we prove that horizontal-only 5×5 Quoridor is a forced second-player win for every wall budget to infinity, discover a sharp single-vertical-wall phase flip at W=(5,5), and solve the complete 16/16 vertical-wall phase grid on 7×7 at W=(3,3) — identifying exact infinite-horizon draw regimes. On the learning side, we train a 2,600.3 Elo 7×7 neural engine across 38,400 self-play games in 52 minutes on a single desktop workstation.

1. Why Quoridor is an ideal scientific testbed

At first glance, Quoridor looks minimal: two pawns race across a grid to reach the opposite edge, and on each turn a player either steps forward or places a two-cell wooden wall to lengthen the opponent's path without sealing them off completely.

Mathematically, Quoridor is a sharp adversarial shortest-path problem. Every wall placement rewrites global graph connectivity across the entire board. A seemingly quiet wall can lock in a forced maze ten plies later, while shifting a single barrier by one square can invert a winning line into a forced loss. That tight coupling of non-local graph topology, resource budgeting, and exact verification makes Quoridor a clean laboratory for testing genuine reasoning and machine-assisted discovery.

Figure 1. A single vertical wall (white) breaks horizontal parity and inverts the exact game-theoretic outcome.

2. Computer-verified theorems on 5×5 and 7×7

Our first track asks a pure mathematical question: what is the exact game-theoretic truth of Quoridor under perfect play? Using custom C exact proof solvers engineered for high-throughput search, we established a family of computer-verified phase-transition theorems across 5×5 and 7×7 boards:

  • The Horizontal-Only 5×5 Invariant: When only horizontal walls are permitted on a 5×5 board, the second player (BLACK) has a mathematically proven forced win for every symmetric wall budget from 0 to infinity, bounded cleanly because at most 8 non-overlapping horizontal walls can coexist on the 5×5 grid.
  • The Single-Vertical-Wall Phase Flip: At W=(5,5) on 5×5, permitting just one single vertical wall per player shatters horizontal parity and flips the empty-board truth from a forced BLACK win (≤40 plies) to a forced first-player WHITE win (≤42 plies).
  • The Complete 16/16 Phase Law on 7×7: At 3 walls per player on 7×7, we solved all 16 combinations of vertical-wall caps (v0, v1) from the empty board — mapping the exact boundary between forced WHITE wins, forced BLACK wins, and exact infinite-horizon draws where neither side can force progress against optimal defense.
  • Pushing the 7×7 Exact Frontier: We extended exact verification to unrestricted W=(4,3) (forced WHITE win in ≤31 plies), horizontal-only W=(4,4) and W=(5,4), and post-jump exact thresholds where BLACK wins even against an opponent holding unlimited horizontal walls once BLACK holds at least k*=4 walls.

3. Optimizing neural self-play: 2,600+ Elo on a single workstation

Exact provers establish ground truth inside structured regimes, while unrestricted larger boards remain combinatorially vast. In parallel with our exact solvers, we engineered a lean neural self-play and search pipeline for 7×7 Quoridor designed to reach grandmaster strength on a single desktop workstation rather than an industrial GPU cluster.

By pairing a multi-threaded C simulation core with tightly batched GPU evaluation, our compact teacher architecture sustains 230,000+ position evaluations per second in under 350 MB of VRAM, and our full grandmaster network reaches 2,600.3 Elo in 52 minutes (up to 89k evals/sec) — discovering sharp wall sacrifices, maze inversions, and tempo traps entirely from self-play.

4. How this work drives Space AI

Our Quoridor program is a direct prototype of the scientific loop we are scaling across Space AI:

  • Formal discovery + neural intuition: Neural networks narrow vast search spaces quickly; exact solvers and verifiers turn intuition into mathematical certainty.
  • Extreme workstation efficiency: Reaching 2,600+ Elo and multi-million-node proofs on a single desktop shows how much capability comes from clean systems engineering rather than raw cloud spend.
  • Calibrated, open intelligence: Bringing verifiable uncertainty, model efficiency, and collective compute together is the exact foundation running live on the Space AI Network.
