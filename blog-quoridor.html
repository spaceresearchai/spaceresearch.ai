Solving the Maze: Exact Proofs and Neural Grandmasters in Quoridor

Why a deceptively simple wooden board game became one of our sharpest laboratories for exact search, spatial reasoning, and high-efficiency neural self-play.

Why Quoridor?

At first glance, Quoridor looks minimal: two pawns race across a grid to reach the opposite edge, and on each turn a player either steps forward or places a two-cell wooden wall to slow the opponent down without completely sealing them off.

Under the hood, Quoridor is brutally subtle. Every wall rewrites the shortest-path geometry of the entire board. A move that looks passive can trigger a non-local maze trap ten plies later, and a single wall placed one square off can invert a won game into a forced loss. That combination of graph topology, resource scarcity, and adversarial planning makes Quoridor an ideal benchmark for testing genuine reasoning versus pattern memorization.

A single vertical wall (white) breaks horizontal parity and flips the game-theoretic winner.

Exact computer-verified theorems on 5×5 and 7×7

Our first research track asks a pure mathematical question: what is the exact game-theoretic truth of Quoridor under perfect play? Using custom C exact solvers built for high-throughput proof search, we established several computer-verified theorems across 5×5 and 7×7 boards:

  • The Horizontal-Only 5×5 Invariant: When only horizontal walls are permitted on a 5×5 board, the second player (BLACK) has a mathematically proven forced win for every symmetric wall budget from 0 all the way to infinity (since at most 8 non-overlapping horizontal walls can coexist on the board).
  • The Single-Vertical-Wall Phase Flip: At 5 walls per player on 5×5, permitting just one single vertical wall per player shatters horizontal parity and flips the exact empty-board outcome from a forced BLACK win (in at most 40 plies) to a forced first-player WHITE win (in at most 42 plies).
  • The Complete 16/16 Phase Law on 7×7: On the larger 7×7 board at 3 walls per player, we solved all 16 combinations of vertical-wall caps (v0, v1) from the empty board — mapping the exact boundaries between forced WHITE wins, forced BLACK wins, and exact infinite-horizon draws where neither side can force progress against optimal defense.
  • Pushing the 7×7 Exact Frontier: We extended verified proofs to unrestricted W=(4,3) (forced WHITE win in ≤31 plies), horizontal-only W=(4,4) and W=(5,4), and post-jump exact thresholds where BLACK wins even against an opponent with unlimited horizontal walls once BLACK holds at least 4 walls.

2,600+ Elo neural self-play on a single workstation

Exact provers reveal ground truth in structured regimes, but full unrestricted boards still explode combinatorially. Alongside our exact solvers, we built a custom neural self-play and search pipeline for 7×7 Quoridor designed to run entirely on a single desktop machine rather than a giant datacenter cluster.

How Quoridor connects back to Space AI

Quoridor is not a side distraction — it is a proving ground for the exact principles behind Space AI:

  • Zero-bluff verification: You cannot sweet-talk a theorem prover or a shortest-path maze. Either a line holds under adversarial scrutiny or it collapses.
  • Maximum intelligence per watt on local hardware: Pushing 2,600+ Elo training and multi-million-node exact proofs on a single desktop workstation proves how far consumer hardware can go when software is engineered cleanly.
  • Search meets intuition: Combining fast neural intuition with calibrated uncertainty and verification is the same bridge we are building into Space AI's Mind Window and open network.
