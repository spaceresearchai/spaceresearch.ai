What We Are Building at Space AI

AI is quickly becoming the basic substrate of thinking, coding, and discovery. Yet almost all of it runs inside closed black boxes owned by a handful of cloud landlords. We are building the open alternative.

1. Open intelligence on everyday hardware

Millions of capable Apple Silicon Macs and NVIDIA workstations sit idle for twenty hours a day. Meanwhile, developers hit sudden cloud usage caps and pay recurring subscriptions to rent inference from opaque APIs.

Space AI turns everyday computers into a shared, verifiable network for open-weight models. Anyone can open Space Chat in a browser or point an app directly at our open API with zero setup. If you own a Mac or gaming PC, one command lets your machine contribute idle time safely — with built-in Auto-Pause that yields the instant you start gaming or working.

2. Making certainty visible instead of hiding doubt

Closed AI APIs deliver wild guesswork in the exact same calm, authoritative voice as rock-solid facts. You never get to see whether the model was 99% certain or flipping a coin between five competing answers.

Because Space AI runs directly on open weights, we stream live confidence on every response. When a model hesitates or bluffs, you see it immediately — and in our upcoming desktop and local routing tools, low confidence can automatically trigger a retry or escalate to a stronger model before a bad guess breaks your code.

3. Verified models and protected Home Orbitals

Open compute networks have historically struggled with two quiet failure modes: dishonest providers silently swapping in a smaller, cheaper model, and massive industrial data-center farms drowning out individual contributors.

We address both directly. Active stations are checked continuously in the background to confirm they are serving the genuine open weights they advertise, and stations that fail are automatically dropped from routing. At the same time, everyday Macs and gaming PCs are grouped into protected Home Orbitals separated from industrial farms so independent builders always earn fairly.

4. Built for real daily workflows

We do not want people to install heavy infrastructure just to try Space AI. Start directly in Space Chat or over HTTPS, use our 1-line CLI (space claude) to keep coding smoothly whenever a cloud tool hits a rate limit, or follow our Live Network as we roll out native Space Desktop with local-first spillover.
