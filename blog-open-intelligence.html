Why We Built Space AI: Open Machine Intelligence for Scientific Discovery

Scientific discovery is entering an era where machine intelligence can explore spaces too vast for human intuition alone. Yet today's strongest systems are locked inside closed black boxes, engineered for hyperscale cloud rent rather than verifiable truth. Space AI exists to build the open scientific alternative.

THESIS SUMMARY

We focus on four connected research directions: (1) pairing neural intuition with formal verification to discover new mathematics and science, (2) optimizing models for extreme efficiency and live uncertainty inspection, (3) pulling multiple models and everyday computers together into one collective intelligence, and (4) keeping our weights, proofs, and infrastructure open.

1. Discovering new science with machine intelligence

Real science does not reward plausible-sounding prose; it rewards claims that survive contact with reality and proof. Much of modern AI optimizes for fluent conversation while quietly hiding uncertainty or hallucinating citations.

At Space AI, we treat machine intelligence as a scientific instrument. We pair fast neural guidance with exact formal search, theorem provers, and structured verification so systems can uncover genuinely new facts, from computer-verified phase-transition theorems in adversarial graph games like Quoridor to broader mathematical and algorithmic discovery. When a machine proposes a conjecture or strategy, we want the proof or counterexample right beside it.

2. Optimizing intelligence, doing vastly more per watt

Progress in AI is often treated as a brute-force race to build larger private datacenters. We think software, algorithmic architecture, and mathematical insight still leave orders of magnitude of efficiency on the table.

Our engineering discipline targets maximum intelligence per watt on accessible hardware. In our neural self-play research, compact C simulation cores paired with tightly batched GPU evaluation push over 230,000 position evaluations per second inside 350 MB of memory, training grandmaster-strength agents in under an hour on a single desktop workstation.

Optimization also means making inference glass-box instead of black-box. Because we run directly on open weights, we stream live token-level certainty and competing alternatives on every reply inside our Mind Window. Exposing where a model is locked in versus where it is guessing lets both humans and automated verifiers catch errors before they compound.

3. Pulling intelligence together

No single model (and no single machine) holds all the answers. Strong scientific reasoning comes from combining specialized perspectives, cross-checking claims, and pooling compute where it is needed.

Through the Space AI Network, we pull intelligence together along two axes:

  • Collective model reasoning: Our multi-node Swarm Council fans hard prompts across independent stations in parallel and synthesizes stronger, cross-checked answers rather than relying on a single blind sample.
  • Pooled everyday hardware: Millions of Apple Silicon Macs and NVIDIA workstations sit idle for twenty hours a day. By coordinating sharded large-model inference across pooled memory and continuously checking every node for genuine open weights, everyday hardware becomes a shared scientific compute commons, with built-in Auto-Pause so contributors never lose their own machine while working.

4. Open science, open weights, open infrastructure

Science advances fastest when anyone can inspect an instrument, reproduce an experiment, and build on prior results without asking permission from a cloud monopoly.

Every layer of Space AI is designed to stay open and auditable: open-weight models, continuous background model authenticity checks across our Live Network, protected Home Orbitals that keep individual contributors independent from industrial server farms, and zero-friction tools, from direct HTTPS APIs and Space Chat to our 1-line CLI (space claude).
