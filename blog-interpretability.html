Glass-Box Chat: Live Interpretability and Causal Interventions in Space Chat

Most interpretability tools live inside offline notebooks long after a model has already shipped. We are building live mechanistic and behavioral instrumentation directly into Space Chat so every reply can be inspected, perturbed, and stress-tested in real time.

ABSTRACT

Closed chat interfaces collapse a rich probability distribution over internal reasoning trajectories into a single confident stream of text. Inside Space Chat, we expose four real-time diagnostic instruments running on open weights: (1) token-level decision geometry (probability, top-2 logit margin, and local entropy), (2) 1-click counterfactual token forking for causal trajectory intervention, (3) antisymmetric paired-swap auditing that cancels out prompt-order bias in ~215 ms to separate factual grounding, sycophancy, and overconfident bluffing, and (4) 5-framing belief stability stress-tests plus cycle-free pairwise comparison.

1. Why interpretability belongs inside the live interface

When a language model answers a scientific or engineering question, the visible sentence is only the final surface projection of thousands of internal competitions. A token chosen with 99.8% certainty and a token chosen by a 51% to 49% coin flip render in the exact same font and confident tone.

At Space AI, we treat chat not just as a consumer text box, but as an interactive laboratory instrument. Because we serve open-weight models directly on community hardware, we can inspect the model's exact next-token decision geometry, intervene causally mid-generation, and run fast parallel diagnostic probes on the same live silicon.

2. Decision X-Ray: exposing next-token geometry

As every reply streams in Space Chat, our Decision X-Ray records three microscopic quantities at every token index t:

  • Chosen probability (p): How much probability mass the model placed on the emitted token.
  • Top-2 decision margin (m): The log-odds gap between the winning token and its closest runner-up. When m collapses below 0.6 (or p < 0.55), the model is standing on a sharp cliff edge where a tiny perturbation changes the trajectory.
  • Local Shannon entropy (H) and runner-up branches: Whether probability mass is split cleanly between two coherent hypotheses or smeared across many uncertain guesses.

We keep the default reading surface quiet — showing a single clean confidence number per reply — while letting you toggle Inspect on any message to light up exact fork points and click any individual word to examine the competing candidates the model almost emitted.

3. 1-click counterfactual token forking

Observing uncertainty is passive; true mechanistic work requires causal intervention. Clicking any highlighted token in Space Chat opens its competing probability distribution and lets you click any runner-up alternative to fork reality at that exact token boundary.

Space Chat preserves the exact prefix up to token t - 1, transplants your chosen alternative at position t, and streams the counterfactual continuation live from that branch point. This makes two failure modes immediately visible in practice:

  • Fragile reasoning cascades: A single low-margin token early in a proof or explanation (such as choosing however versus therefore) can pivot the entire downstream conclusion into an opposite answer.
  • Strong factual attractors: When a model genuinely knows a fact, forcing a different phrasing early in the sentence still converges back to the exact same core claim.

4. Antisymmetric paired-swap auditing (cancelling position bias)

Asking a language model to judge whether an answer is honest or sycophantic is notoriously noisy because models exhibit strong prompt-order bias — systematically preferring Option A over Option B regardless of content.

To eliminate this artifact analytically, Space Chat's background audit (/v1/audit) evaluates three orthogonal diagnostic axes in parallel — Factual Grounding, Sycophancy / Pandering, and Overconfident Bluffing — by running every binary probe twice with swapped option order, (A, B) and (B, A).

Given the raw A-vs-B log-odds margins m_AB and m_BA, we decompose them into two exact components:

  • Order-invariant antisymmetric signal: s = 0.5 * (m_AB - m_BA) — any constant preference for the first or second slot cancels identically to zero.
  • Pure position-bias residual: b = 0.5 * (m_AB + m_BA) — isolated and discarded so it cannot corrupt the calibration readout.

Because all six single-token probes execute concurrently on the local GPU runtime, a full de-biased audit finishes in roughly 215 milliseconds, flagging sycophancy, fabrication risk, or uncalibrated bluffing without slowing down conversation.

5. 5-framing belief stability and cycle-free comparisons

Two additional built-in commands inside Space Chat extend this same de-biased methodology to interactive behavioral experiments:

  • Belief stability stress-testing (/stress): Automatically detects Yes/No or A-vs-B questions and re-asks the model across five graded social-pressure framings (Push Left, Lean Left, Neutral, Lean Right, Push Right). Each response is scored with a paired-swap de-biased readout so you can measure exact maximum belief swing and see whether user pressure causes the model to flip its stance.
  • Cycle-free pairwise comparison (/compare): Evaluates every ordered pair (i, j) and (j, i) across a set of competing options, cancels position bias via antisymmetric margins, and reports both the raw position-bias rate and the exact number of intransitivity cycles (A > B > C > A) eliminated from the final ranking.

6. Where we are taking this next

Token-level decision geometry, causal counterfactual forking, and antisymmetric paired-swap probes are the first live layer of our interpretability program — focused on what can be measured cleanly and cheaply at interactive speed on everyday hardware.

Next, we are bridging these live behavioral and token-space instruments deeper into internal model circuits: pairing low-margin fork points and sycophancy flips with layer-wise representation probes, activation-steering checks, and automatic certainty-triggered escalation across our open Space AI Network. The goal is simple: machine intelligence you can inspect down to the mechanism, not take on faith.
